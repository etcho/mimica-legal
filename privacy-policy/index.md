# Mímica - Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 16, 2026
**Version:** 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Android app "Mímica", also published as "Mime Game - Try to Guess" (package `com.etcho.mimica`, the "App"), processes, why, and what rights you have.

## Summary

- **You do not need an account, and the names of your teams and players never leave your device.** Neither does the word drawn for each turn.
- The App shows a banner ad and, if you choose to watch one, a rewarded video from **Google AdMob**. Where the law requires it (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland and similar), we ask for your consent first.
- You can buy **Remove ads** once, through **Google Play**. Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details.
- The App uses **Google Analytics for Firebase** and **Firebase Crashlytics** to learn how the game is played and to fix crashes. They receive device and usage information, **never the names you type or the words drawn**.
- We never sell your data.

## 2. Data stored only on your device

- **Your game:** team names, player names, the score, the categories and mode you chose, the words already drawn and a game in progress, so the game goes on when you open the App again.
- **Your settings:** mime time, sounds on or off, the App's language, and whether you have seen the tutorial.
- **Rating prompt:** the App counts how many times it was opened and whether it has already asked you to rate it, so it asks only once.
- **Remove ads:** whether you bought it, as Google Play last confirmed, so the App starts without ads right away.
- **Mímica PRO:** the App checks, on your device only, whether the old paid app "Mímica PRO" is installed; if it is, the App shows no ads. This check is not sent anywhere.
- This data is stored only in the App's private storage on your device.
- **Android device backup:** depending on your device settings, Android may include this data in your device's own backup to your Google account. This backup is managed by Google and your device settings, not by us.

We do not have access to this data. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

The App does not collect your location, contacts, photos, files or any account information. Its only special permission is the one Google Play requires for in-app purchases.

## 3. Advertising

- The App displays a banner ad provided by **Google AdMob**. When you ask for a third word in the same turn, it offers a **rewarded video** that you can choose to watch, or not.
- AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address and information about the ads shown and clicked, as an independent controller under Google's policies.
- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where else required by law, we ask for your consent before ads are requested, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the App's menu (⋮) > **Privacy options** (shown where this choice applies). If you do not consent, the App keeps working normally, and any ads you see are not personalized.
- If you buy **Remove ads** (Section 4) or have Mímica PRO installed, the App requests no ads at all.
- On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
- **Your team and player names and the words drawn are never shared with AdMob or used to select ads.**
- More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 4. In-app purchase (Remove ads)

- **Remove ads** is a one-time purchase made through **Google Play Billing**. Google Play processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy, and tells the App only whether the purchase was made, is pending or was refunded.
- We never receive your name, email, card or other payment details.
- The App asks Google Play about your purchase when it opens, so it also works on your other devices with the same Google account.
- Google Analytics for Firebase automatically records that a purchase happened (product, price and currency), to count sales.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the purchase you asked for (GDPR art. 6(1)(b); LGPD art. 7, V).

## 5. Analytics and crash reports

- We use **Google Analytics for Firebase** to understand how the App is used, and **Firebase Crashlytics** to receive reports about crashes and errors.
- These services process an App instance identifier, device and operating system information, approximate location derived from the IP address (country and region), and usage events.
- Usage events describe the game, **never the names you typed or the word drawn**: for example, "a game started in Competition mode with 3 teams and 5 categories", "a turn ended with a correct guess in the Action category with 40 seconds left", "a word was drawn again after a video", "a competition was won", "the Remove ads purchase screen was opened from the menu", "the App was shared".
- Analytics **does not use the advertising ID**.
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in keeping the App working and improving it (GDPR art. 6(1)(f); LGPD art. 7, IX), or your consent where the law requires it.
- **Retention:** analytics data is kept for at most 14 months, and crash reports for up to 90 days.

## 6. Links that leave the App

The menu can open the App's page on Google Play (to rate it), Google Play's rating window inside the App, Google Play's purchase window, Android's share sheet (to share a link to the App) and this policy in your browser. What happens there is governed by Google Play, by the app you choose to share with and by your browser, under their own policies. The App does not receive anything back, except whether a purchase was made; Google Play does not tell us whether or how you rated the App.

## 7. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

| Provider | Service | Data | Role |

| Google (Google Play Billing) | In-app purchase | Your purchase and payment, in your Google account | Independent controller for payments, see Section 4 |

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to be informed about with whom data is shared.

- **European Union / EEA / UK (GDPR / UK GDPR):** you may also complain to your local data protection authority.
- **Brazil (LGPD, art. 18):** you may also complain to the ANPD (Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados).
- **United States (California and other states with consumer privacy laws):** you have the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Categories collected: identifiers (App instance ID, advertising ID, IP address), commercial information (the Remove ads purchase), internet or electronic activity (App usage events) and approximate geolocation (from IP).

The App has no account, so we cannot link the analytics or crash data to you by your name or email. To exercise a right, email **etcho.apps@gmail.com**; we will answer within the period required by law. For ads, you can also use the choices in Section 3; for purchases, your Google Play account.

## 9. Age

The App is a party game for groups, for a general audience. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child's data was collected, contact us.

## 10. Security

The App sends data only to the Google services above, over encrypted connections (TLS). The names you type and the words drawn never leave your device.
